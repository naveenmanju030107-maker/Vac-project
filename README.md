# AI Technology Trends Report 2025–2026

## About the Project
This project presents a report on Artificial Intelligence Technology Trends for the years 2025–2026. It explores emerging AI technologies, their applications, and their impact on various industries.

- To explore emerging AI technologies.
- To study real-world applications of AI.
- To understand the future scope of Artificial Intelligence.
